# Common Cold

## Overview
The common cold is a mild viral infection of the upper respiratory tract, affecting the nose and throat. Many different viruses can cause a cold, with rhinoviruses being the most frequent.

## Common Symptoms
- Runny or stuffy nose and sneezing.
- Sore or scratchy throat.
- Cough and mild body aches.
- Mild fatigue or a low-grade fever.
Symptoms usually develop 1 to 3 days after exposure and often improve within 7 to 10 days.

## General Comfort Measures
- Get adequate rest to support recovery.
- Stay well hydrated with water, warm tea, or broth.
- Use a cool-mist humidifier or saline nasal spray to help ease congestion.
- Wash hands frequently and cover coughs and sneezes to reduce spreading the virus to others.

## When to Seek Medical Care
Consult a healthcare professional if symptoms last longer than 10 days without improvement, worsen after starting to improve, or are accompanied by high fever, shortness of breath, wheezing, chest pain, or severe sinus pain.
